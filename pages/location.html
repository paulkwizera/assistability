Directory of Organisations

National Union of Disability Organizations of Rwanda (NUDOR)

Coordinates and represents disability organizations across Rwanda, promoting equal rights and inclusion.

Location: Niboye, Kicukiro, Kigali

Phone: +250 788 845 444

Rwanda National Union of the Deaf (RNUD)

Provides sign language advocacy, education, and support services for deaf people and their families.

Location: Kagarama, Kicukiro, Kigali

Phone: +250 788 209 212

Rwanda Union of the Blind (RUB)

Supports people with visual impairments through education, rehabilitation, advocacy, and employment programs.

Location: Kigali

Phone: +250 788 856 671

UPHLS

Works to improve health, inclusion, employment, and well-being of persons with disabilities throughout Rwanda.

Location: Kimihurura, Kigali

Tubiteho NGO

Supports children and adults with disabilities through education, healthcare, and community development projects.

Location: Kigali

Rwanda Union of Little People (RULP)

Promotes equal opportunities and supports people with short stature through advocacy, education, and empowerment.

Location: Kimironko, Kigali
