How to Help

Learn simple and practical ways to support people with disabilities and help build a more inclusive community.

❤️ Respect Everyone

  • Treat everyone with kindness and respect.
  • Speak directly to the person, not only to their caregiver.
  • Ask before offering help.
  • Respect personal space and independence.
  • Listen carefully to their needs.

👨‍🦯 Helping Someone with a Visual Impairment

  • Introduce yourself when approaching.
  • Offer your arm instead of pulling the person.
  • Describe the surroundings when requested.
  • Tell the person when you are leaving.
  • Keep walkways free from obstacles.

🤟 Helping Someone Who Is Deaf or Hard of Hearing

  • Face the person while speaking.
  • Speak clearly without shouting.
  • Use gestures or write messages if needed.
  • Learn basic sign language.
  • Be patient during conversations.

🦽 Helping Someone Using a Wheelchair

  • Never push a wheelchair without permission.
  • Keep ramps and pathways clear.
  • Ensure entrances are accessible.
  • Offer assistance only when asked.
  • Sit at eye level during long conversations.

🧠 Supporting People with Intellectual or Learning Disabilities

  • Use simple and clear language.
  • Give enough time to understand information.
  • Be patient and encouraging.
  • Avoid making assumptions about their abilities.

🚑 During an Emergency

  • Stay calm.
  • Ask how you can help.
  • Follow emergency instructions.
  • Help people reach safe exits if requested.
  • Never leave someone behind if they need assistance.

✅ Accessibility Checklist

  • Accessible entrances and ramps.
  • Wide doorways.
  • Accessible toilets.
  • Braille signs where possible.
  • Good lighting.
  • Captioned videos.
  • Wheelchair parking spaces.

❤️ Become a Volunteer

You can make a difference by:

  • Helping someone travel to appointments.
  • Teaching computer skills.
  • Reading books aloud.
  • Teaching sign language.
  • Donating assistive equipment.
  • Participating in community events.

❓ Myths and Facts

Myth: People with disabilities always need help.

Fact: Many people are independent and only need assistance in certain situations.


Myth: Disability means inability.

Fact: People with disabilities can study, work, play sports, and achieve their goals.

🌍 Why Inclusion Matters

An inclusive community gives everyone equal opportunities to learn, work, travel, and participate in society regardless of their abilities. Small acts of kindness can make a big difference.

💙 Remember

"The greatest barrier is not a person's disability, but society's attitude toward disability."
